# Art bible

Status: style A (painted) chosen by James 2026-10-02. Characters below are proposals until James approves the round 2 sheets.

## Style lock (every prompt; `assets-in/style-a-painted.jpg` is the reference image)
Rich hand-painted fantasy storybook illustration, gouache and oil textures with visible brushwork and soft painterly edges, light dark outlines on characters, glowing warm light against cool moonlit blues, deep atmosphere, the feel of a classic illustrated adventure novel.

Kyle as painted in the reference (canonical): messy dark-brown hair with one blue streak, sea-blue eyes, light freckles, sleeveless teal wrap tunic, blue wave-pattern sash with trailing ends, white wrapped forearms, dark knee-length trousers, barefoot, nautilus-shell pendant on a cord.

## Format rules (every image)
- Page art: portrait 2:3 (ChatGPT 1024×1536). The 3D page is built at 2:3 to match, so nothing is cropped.
- Push-in scenes (p05 dragons, p06 lake-dive, p12 waterfall, p17 final-battle): also a landscape 3:2 (1536×1024) version; prompts come in round 3.
- No text, letters, logos, borders, frames, signatures or watermarks in any image; typography is added by the book.
- Kid-friendly adventure: action without injury or gore. Fire soldiers are knocked down, splashed, trapped in water bubbles; nobody is hurt on-page.
- Keep a calm area in each composition (sky, water, shadow) so the image breathes; no important detail at the outer 5% (page edges get vignetted).

## World
- **Water tribe home**: a village of stilt houses on a jade jungle lake. Woven reed roofs, sky-blue sailcloth awnings, carved wooden wave-spiral totems, rope bridges between houses, lanterns made of glass floats. Rainforest all around, waterfalls in the hills.
- **Water tribe emblem**: a spiral wave inside a circle (carved on totems, worn as a shell pendant).
- **Fire tribe**: from volcanic badlands. Soldiers wear charcoal-black armour with glowing ember-orange seams, bronze shoulder plates, helmets with a flame-shaped crest; they throw fireballs. Their camp (ch3): black tents, iron fire braziers, banners with a flame-eye emblem.
- **Dragons**: house-sized, lean, crimson-and-charcoal scales with molten cracks that glow, smoky breath, wings with glowing veins, golden eyes. Held on heavy chains by soldiers.
- **Amazon River**: wide brown-green river, giant lily pads, mangrove roots, fireflies at night, mist at dawn.
