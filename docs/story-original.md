Story Title: Water Born

Chapter 1: Under Attack

Once upon a time, there were two tribes: Water and Fire. Here is how the story starts.

*Boom!*
*Crsh!!*

The roof was slowly starting to go down. The ground beneath me was rumbling - We were under attack... again. I grabbed my family's last rations of food and leaped out of the house. All I can see was fire. Trying to put out the fires with my water power was no use. We were forced to flee but I needed to put up a good fight first.

I went in with a diving uppercut, catching a soldier off guard. Then they brought out the dragons. I jumped into the lake knowing I might never see this place again. We settled down at the Amazon River. I thought that night... I need to bring back our home. Lucky I had a group of resistence fighters willing to possibly risk their lives to bring back our home.

We started by gathering resources and learning new moves. We were ready to go. Oh wait... Let me introduce ourselves. I am Kyle of the Water tribe, and this is Logan, Ruby, Zoro, and Flora.

Back to the story.

It was evening as we decided to set off.

---

Chapter 2: Rise of Water

We swam down the river's twists and turns until...

*SPLASH!*

Up ahead there was a waterfall! We went the wrong way! We needed to act fast.

Just then, we spotted vines at the top of the waterfall!

One.. Two... And three!

We leaped onto the vines. When the moon was at its highest in the sky, we got close to our home. We needed a goodnight sleep to be prepared for tomorrow.

Chapter 3: The Fall of Fire

In the morning we snuck into the camp. Picking off the guards one by one. When we were putting out the fires one of the guards spotted us. As he came closer to us, we put out the last fire. There were 3 fire people left after the battle. We put all we had into that fight and we won. We can now live peacefully in the jungle.

The End.
